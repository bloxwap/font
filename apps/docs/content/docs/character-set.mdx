Glyphs include alternates, ligatures, small caps and other forms reached through [OpenType features](/docs/opentype-features); characters are the Unicode code points the font maps.

## What is covered

- **Latin**: Western and Central European, Baltic, Turkish, Vietnamese, Pan-African Latin and IPA.
- **Greek**: monotonic Greek.
- **Cyrillic**: Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Bulgarian, Serbian, Macedonian, plus Central Asian and Caucasian letters.
- **Figures and currency**: tabular and proportional figures, superiors, inferiors, fractions, and currency signs including ₿ € ₹ ₽.
- **Symbols**: math, arrows, box drawing, block elements and interface symbols.

Arabic, Armenian, Georgian, Hebrew, Japanese kana and kanji, Hangul and hanzi are in the [companion families](/docs/multi-script).

## Glyph browser

Every glyph in the font, rendered by the font. Choose a family, filter by category or script, or search by character (`é`), glyph name (`Aacute`), code point (`U+00E9`, `0xE9`) or decimal (`233`).

- Click a glyph, or press **Enter**, to copy it.
- Move through the grid with the **arrow keys**; **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last glyph.
- Glyphs marked with a dot are reached through an OpenType feature; the inspector names it.
- **Use in tester** opens the glyph in the type tester on the home page.
