# Conventions
#   青-top: 龶 (青 责 素 毒 麦 表…)     牛-top: ⺧ (告 先 生-less)    羊-top: ⺶ (美 姜 羔)
#   ナ (左 右 有 布 友): 𠂇           丿一 top (矢 午 每 乞 年-less): 𠂉
#   flat-丿 + 丿 (反 后 质 盾): 𠂆    ⺈ = 刀-like top of 负 角 争 色 免 危 象 急
#   ⺊ = 卜-like top (卢 卓 桌)      ⺌ = 小-top (当 尚 光 肖 党)  ⺍ = 学/觉/举 top
#   ⺮ = 竹 top   爫 = 爪 top   氺 = 水 bottom (录 泰 求-less)   乂 (not 㐅)
#   Positional variants (木.L, 土.L, 王.L, 心.B …) are chosen by the engine; IDS use base names.
#
# Encoded characters with no stroke data in Unihan (declared here; han_check does not yet
# parse bare-char declarations — suggest it also accept `# X=N` for single code points):
#   𠂉=2  丿 over short 一 (top of 矢 午 乞 每)
#   𠂇=2  ナ: 一 + 丿 (左 右 有 布 友)
#   𠂆=2  short flat 丿 over long 丿, 厂-like (反 后 质 盾)
#   ⺈=2  丿 + 横钩 (top of 负 争 色 角 免 危 象)
#   ⺊=2  丨 + short 一 to the right (top of 卢 卓 桌)
#   ⺌=3  丨 flanked by 丶 丿 (top of 当 尚 光 肖)
#   ⺍=3  three dots (top of 学 觉 举 誉)
#   ⺧=4  牛 without its lower tail (top of 告 先)
#   ⺮=6  竹 top
#   ⺶=6  丷 + 王 (top of 美 姜 羔)
#   𠃌=1  横折钩 (right of 幻)
#   𡗗=5  三 + 人 (top of 春 奏 泰 秦)
#   ⺀=2  two stacked dots (inside 尽)
#   𠬝=4  卩 with 又 tucked under (right of 报 服)
#   ⺆=2  丿 + 横折钩 frame (outer of 周)
#   𢦏=6  十 under 戈's frame (outer of 哉 栽 载) — same as ids_b
#   𠂢=6  right part of 派 (reversed 永 without dot)
#

#   {丨丿}=2  short 丨 + 丿, left part of 归 帅 师
